RACI definitions

R — ResponsibleDoes the work to complete the task. One or more per task.
A — AccountableOwns the outcome and signs off. Exactly one per task.
C — ConsultedTwo-way input before decisions or work. Their feedback is sought.
I — InformedKept up to date on progress and outcomes. One-way.

How this aligns with PMI & Agile

  • PMI (PMBOK): starter roles follow classic PMI stakeholders — Project Sponsor, Project Manager, Steering Committee, SME, QA, Business Analyst, Change Control Board.
  • Agile (Scrum Guide): Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team, plus supporting roles (UX, DevOps). Mix them freely — each project picks its own role set.
  • External stakeholders: the External "Customer Point of Contact" role represents the customer-side person you speak to about product deliverables, and the channel from whom you listen to and collect feedback. Map a different person to it per project.
  • Golden rules enforced: exactly one A per task, at least one R per task, a person may play multiple roles, and a role may stay unassigned (blank).

Data, backup & sharing

  • Your data lives in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere. It persists between visits on the same browser.
  • Backup downloads a JSON file with all projects and the full audit trail. Import backup restores it (replaces current data).
  • Hand the app to a friend and they start fresh (empty storage). Give them your backup file and they can import it to start where you are.
  • Every change is recorded in the Audit Log for historical reference, and is included in the Excel export.

Exports

  • Excel (.xlsx) — 3 sheets: color-coded RACI Matrix, Roles & People, Audit Log.
  • PNG image — a shareable picture of the matrix with colors and legend.
